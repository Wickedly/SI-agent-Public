Personal AI operator

Private assistance, controlled by the owner.

SI-Agent is a self-hosted personal-assistant project. It helps its authorized owner review information from connected services, understand important changes, and receive notifications through a messaging interface.

What SI-Agent does

  • Gmail: uses owner-authorized, read-only access to answer questions about mail and, when monitoring is enabled, identify messages that may need attention.
  • Google Calendar: reads selected event details to answer scheduling questions and may report relevant changes when monitoring is enabled.
  • Local assessment: processes permitted information on a self-hosted system and records operational history for reliability and auditability.
  • Notifications: may send selected summaries or alerts through a configured messaging service such as Telegram.

Owner control and limitations

Google access requires authorization from the account owner. Reading messages or calendar events does not authorize SI-Agent to send email, change calendar events, approve requests, make payments, or perform other consequential actions.

This is a personal-use project, not a public Gmail or Calendar service. Automated classifications may be delayed, incomplete, or incorrect. Confirm important information in its original service.

About your data. This public website does not connect to Google accounts or collect Gmail and Calendar content. The separately hosted SI-Agent application may keep local records needed for the enabled features. See the Privacy Policy.

Contact and project identity

SI-Agent is maintained by the independent developer account Wickedly on GitHub. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google or Telegram. For support or privacy inquiries, contact the maintainer through that profile. Do not post account credentials or sensitive data in public GitHub issues.
